Also note anything you looked at and decided was **not** a defect, and why:
- **`emp_code` pattern `^EMP\d{4,6}$`:** Looked at whether `emp_code` should be strictly 4 digits (e.g. `EMP0001`). The schema and sample data permit 4 to 6 digits (`EMP0001` through `EMP999999`), which is valid per contract.
- **MongoDB BSON `Date` storage vs Epoch Milliseconds in API:** Evaluated whether MongoDB documents should store timestamps as raw integers or BSON datetimes. BSON UTC datetimes in MongoDB are intentional per `DATA_MODEL.md` (samples 1–9) and enable native date aggregation operators; converting to epoch milliseconds occurs during API serialization.
- **Natural key `(emp_code, date)` uniqueness:** Examined if an employee could have multiple attendance records per date. Rule R1 explicitly states a record's date is the IST calendar date of its punch-in, and an employee has at most one attendance record per attendance date.
